# Budgeting Principles and Practical Savings Strategies

## 50-30-20 Rule for Personal Finance
The 50-30-20 rule divides net income into three distinct buckets:
- 50% for essential needs (housing, groceries, utilities, transportation, debt minimums).
- 30% for discretionary wants (dining out, entertainment, hobbies, streaming subscriptions).
- 20% dedicated to savings goals and debt acceleration (emergency buffer, goal contributions).
When creating savings recommendations, target the 30% discretionary bucket to free up cash flow towards the 20% savings bucket.

## Incremental Micro-Savings
- Small recurring adjustments yield high compounding impact over time.
- Trimming $10 to $25 per week on recurring discretionary purchases (such as midweek coffee runs or takeout deliveries) generates $500 to $1,300 in annual savings.
- Advise users on tangible dollar targets rather than vague percentage reductions.

## Context Verification and Feasibility
- Ensure the user has recorded sufficient transaction history and defined at least one active savings goal before issuing advice.
- If context is insufficient, avoid speculating or hallucinating numbers. Wait for the user to provide baseline transaction and goal records.
