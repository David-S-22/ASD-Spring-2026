# Transactions category guide

## Fitness
Fitness classes, personal training, and fitness club direct debits.
Not for one-off sports equipment purchases (use the relevant shopping category).
Examples: Anytime Fitness Ultimo (Direct debit membership fee).

## Gym memberships
Recurring gym membership fees billed monthly or fortnightly.
Not for fitness classes or personal training (use Fitness).
Examples: GymCo (GymCo membership).

## Music subscriptions
Recurring music streaming plans.
Not for video or general streaming services (use Video subscriptions or Streaming subscriptions).
Examples: Spotify AU (Spotify Premium subscription, Monthly subscription).

## Streaming subscriptions
Recurring general streaming plans that are not video- or music-specific.
Not for dedicated video-on-demand or music services (use Video subscriptions or Music subscriptions).
Examples: Netflix (Netflix.com subscription).

## Video subscriptions
Recurring video-on-demand plans.
Not for general streaming bundles (use Streaming subscriptions).
Examples: Prime Video (Amazon Prime Video).

## Housing
Rent and mortgage payments to a landlord, agent or lender.
Not for household bills such as electricity or internet (use Utilities or Internet).
Examples: Harbourview Realty (Rent payment).

## Internet
Home internet plans.
Not for mobile phone plans (use Mobile).
Examples: FibreLink (Internet plan).

## Mobile
Mobile phone plans.
Not for home internet plans (use Internet).
Examples: Telco One (Mobile plan).

## Utilities
Electricity, gas and water bills.
Not for internet or mobile plans (use Internet or Mobile).
Examples: Sparkwell Energy (Electricity bill).

## Transport
Public transport top-ups, rideshare and fuel.
Not for travel accommodation or flights.
Examples: Transport card (Opal top-up), Sydney Trains (Opal card top-up).

## Shared household
Transfers to housemates or a shared kitty.
Not for rent paid directly to a landlord or agent (use Housing).
Examples: Housemates (Shared kitty transfer).

## Cloud storage
Recurring cloud storage plans.
Not for music, video or streaming subscriptions.
Examples: DriveBox (Cloud storage subscription).

## Dining
Restaurants, cafes, takeaway and food delivery.
Not for supermarket or grocery shopping (use Groceries).
Examples: Merivale (Dinner, Lunch), Chat Thai (Lunch, Dinner).

## Groceries
Supermarkets and grocery stores.
Not for restaurants, cafes or takeaway (use Dining).
Examples: Woolworths (Weekly groceries).

## Uncategorised
System default when nothing else fits. Prefer a specific category when the merchant matches any section above.
Examples: Mystery Merchant (Unrecognised card charge).
