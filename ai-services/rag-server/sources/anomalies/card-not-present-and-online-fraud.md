# Card-not-present and online fraud

Reference guidance for reviewing online, phone, and other card-not-present (CNP)
charges, where the physical card is not checked and fraud risk is higher.

## Elevated baseline risk
CNP charges carry more risk than in-person purchases because stolen card details
alone are enough to transact. Judge them more cautiously, but do not flag a
charge solely for being online.

## Digital goods and instant delivery
Gift cards, in-game currency, cryptocurrency, and other instantly delivered
digital goods are favoured by fraudsters because they are fast and hard to
reverse. Large or repeated charges in these categories deserve closer review.

## New payee shortly after account changes
An online charge to a brand-new payee soon after a password reset, email change,
or new-device login fits an account-takeover pattern. The combination is a
stronger signal than any single event.

## Mismatched shipping and billing
Purchases where shipping and billing details diverge, or where express shipping
is chosen for high-value goods, are common in CNP fraud. Treat these as mild
signals that strengthen alongside an unusual amount.

## Guidance
Flag CNP charges on concrete traits — risky digital goods, new payees after
account changes, or detail mismatches — not merely because the channel is online.
