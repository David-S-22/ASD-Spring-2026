# Legitimate transactions that are not anomalies

Guidance to reduce false positives. These traits, on their own, usually do not
justify flagging a transaction.

## Recognised recurring merchants
Regular direct debits and subscriptions to well-known merchants (utilities,
telcos, streaming, gyms) are expected and should not be flagged solely for being
recurring.

## Everyday retail amounts
Ordinary retail and grocery amounts, including moderately large but plausible
purchases at recognised merchants, are normal spending.

## Expected large payments
Rent, mortgage, tax, tuition, and known bill payments are legitimately large.
A large amount alone is not suspicious when the merchant and description match a
known obligation.

## Consistent history
A charge that matches the account holder's established pattern with a familiar
merchant is expected, even if the amount is somewhat higher than average.

## Guidance
Only flag a transaction when a concrete, verifiable trait stands out. Prefer not
to flag when the merchant is recognised, the amount is plausible, and the timing
is ordinary.
