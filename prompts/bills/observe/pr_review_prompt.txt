## Rules

1. **Repo is read-only.** No branches, commits, pushes or PR comments. A
   defect goes in the note; I decide what happens next.
2. Every finding cites the verbatim requirement text with its source, and
   the evidence (path / commit / line). Where a requirement is ambiguous,
   say "ambiguous — tutor question" rather than ruling.
3. Separate the tiers: (a) genuine spec or criteria gaps in my work,
   (b) declared deviations that need one justification sentence,
   (c) team-level gaps not attributable to Bills, (d) agreement notes.
   Don't inflate (b)–(d) into (a).
4. Tone: factual and constructive — findings as fixes with effort
   estimates, strongest compliance points first, so the note doubles as
   report material.
5. Date-stamp everything and record the head SHA; teammates push actively.
